# Altitude, Numbers, Risk Acceptance, Closing Deck, and Consistency

## 1. Altitude Control (`C / M / G`)

- **Cruising (`C`) — Owner & Supervisory Board:** Enterprise meaning — capital/regulatory exposure, systemic grip, trend, and governance decision needed. Zero raw test mechanics above the fold.
- **Mid (`M`) — Senior Management & Project Board:** Process/project meaning — which control failed, systemic cause family, dependencies, `accountable_role`, `target_date`, and `closure_evidence`.
- **Ground (`G`) — Head of IA & Audit Record:** Exact control IDs, sample sizes, workpaper loci, unrounded source numbers, and exception counts.

### De-Jargoning & Calibrated Confidence
- Replace local audit jargon with plain banking consequences while keeping precision (e.g., *"second approval on high-value wires"* instead of *"WT-04 control deficiency"*).
- Eliminate stacked qualifiers (*"may potentially"*) and passive concealment (*"it was noted"*). State confidence basis explicitly: *"We verified X in tested items; management represented Y, which remains unverified until Q1 re-test."*

## 2. Sampling Honesty & Presentation Rounding

- **Never extrapolate** judgmental, convenience, or incomplete-frame samples to total bank population exposure. Report all five elements: `tested population`, `sample size`, `exception count`, `observed rate`, and `extrapolation limitation`.
- **Canonical-value consistency (not rigid string cloning):** Counts, currencies, denominators, ratings, `accountable_role`s, and `target_date`s must remain canonically consistent across every report layer. Presentation rounding (e.g., `$286,412` summarized as `~$286K` or `~$0.29M` in a Board brief) is permitted **only** when the unrounded source figure remains traceable in the finding block / Appendix B and rounding does not alter materiality or meaning.

## 3. Root-Cause Mutability & Upstream Holds

- **Approved causes (`cause_status: approved`):** Immutable in the report writer—do not alter or soften.
- **Provisional / disputed causes (`cause_status: provisional` or `disputed`):** Keep explicitly provisional in the report and table; present `Auditor inference` alongside `Management representation` without locking either as an agreed fact.
- **Upstream holds:** Surface any `upstream_holds` (unverified legal currency, missing bank methodology, infeasible interim control, or unauthorized risk acceptance) in the Supervisory Board / CAE layers rather than concealing them.

## 4. GIAS Standard 11.5 Risk-Acceptance Escalation

When management declines to remediate a finding and proposes accepting the risk:
1. Check management's delegated risk-acceptance threshold under bank policy.
2. **Prohibit acceptance** where law/regulation prohibits non-compliance, where exposure exceeds management's delegated authority, or where an unacceptable enterprise/depositor risk lacks compensating safeguards.
3. Refuse issue closure; emit a **Risk-Acceptance Escalation Brief** recording: finding ID & rating, residual exposure, management rationale (`Management representation`), CAE objection/assessment, expiry/re-review trigger, and explicit `decide` ask for the Supervisory Board / authorized Owner tier.

## 5. Closing Meeting Deck & Cross-Layer QC Sweep

- **Closing Deck (Project Board / Executive Exit):** 60–90 min structure — engagement objective & scope limitations → holistic verdict & rating basis → finding walk prioritized by severity (using neutral Condition + Consequence) → action-plan verification (`accountable_role`, `target_date`, `interim_control`, `closure_evidence`) → disputed items & escalation log.
- **Final Cross-Layer QC Checklist:**
  1. Every summary number reconciles canonically to finding blocks and Appendix B (with unrounded figures traceable).
  2. Zero upgraded evidentiary labels (`Management representation` ≠ `Verified fact`).
  3. Zero investigative details or `Unverified allegation` prose in routine outputs.
  4. Zero unverified laws cited as binding criteria.
  5. Every audience-mandatory gate met (Owner decision request; Supervisory Board `note/discuss/decide` ask; Project Board `closure_evidence` criteria).
