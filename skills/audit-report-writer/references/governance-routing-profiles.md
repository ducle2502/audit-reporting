# Configurable Governance Routing Profiles

## 1. Charter & Regulatory Precedence Rule

Organizational governance differs across jurisdictions and bank structures (two-tier boards with Supervisory Board / *Ban Kiểm soát*, one-tier boards with Audit Committee, state-owned vs. private vs. listed joint-stock banks, foreign bank branches). Before routing reports:
- **Bank charter & statutory distribution rules govern first.** If corporate bylaws or banking regulations prohibit sharing engagement-level audit reports directly with shareholders/owners, route only authorized aggregate or board-approved escalation summaries to the Owner tier and route full oversight reports to the Supervisory Board / Audit Committee.
- When no bank-specific routing override is supplied, apply the **Default 4-Tier Bank Routing Profile** below.

## 2. Default 4-Tier Bank Routing Profile

| Tier | Primary Format & Budget | Altitude (`C/M/G`) | Mandatory Elements | Exclude / Route Elsewhere |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Owner of the Bank** *(where charter-authorized)* | 1-Page Capital & Franchise Decision Brief (2 min) | Cruising+ (`C` only) | Holistic verdict; capital, regulatory license, P&L, and reputational exposure; management grip; GIAS 11.5 risk-acceptance escalations; **explicit decision request** | Sample mechanics, control IDs, procedural narrative, low-severity items, investigative leads |
| **2. Head of the Supervisory Board** | 1-Page Executive Summary / Oversight Memo (2–5 min) | Cruising (`C` + selective `M`, zero `G` above fold) | Verdict in sentence 1; top 3 consequence themes; systemic root-cause patterns (including recurring `Medium` clusters); `Scope limitation` impacts; overdue/repeat Highs; neutral referral count if authorized; **explicit `note / discuss / decide` ask** | Raw test procedures, unextrapolated sample percentages presented as population facts, undefined internal acronyms, investigative evidence |
| **3. Head of Internal Audit (CAE)** | Full Bank Assurance Report Structure Aligned with GIAS Standard 15.1 | All layers (`C + M + G` + Record) | Complete GIAS 15.1 baseline elements; holistic conclusion rationale across all 6 dimensions; scope, exclusions & limitations; conformance statement; full 5-Cs blocks with `cause_status` & `upstream_holds`; prior-issue table; detailed test & sample appendix; AI/canonical QC sign-off | Negotiated/rule-free ratings, upgraded evidentiary labels, unverified legal citations presented as binding |
| **4. Project Board** | Short-Form Remediation Pack / Summary Table + Closing Deck (10–30 min) | Mid + Ground (`M + G`) | Canonical Summary of Findings table; control & delivery gaps; cross-team dependencies; `accountable_role`; exact `target_date`; active `interim_control` (or infeasibility escalation); **explicit `closure_evidence` criteria** | Governance-only boilerplate, unrelated corporate history, investigative referral details |

## 3. Mandatory Output Gates by Audience

- **Owner Decision Brief Gate:** Must contain an explicit **Decision Requested** block naming the decision options, consequence of inaction, and deadline (never passive "for information only" when an escalation or risk acceptance requires principal action).
- **Supervisory Board Oversight Gate:** Must contain an explicit **Governance Ask** (`note`, `discuss`, or `decide`), clearly state any `Scope limitation` affecting assurance, and elevate recurring `Medium` patterns where systemic.
- **Project Board Remediation Gate:** Every action item must include `accountable_role`, `target_date`, `interim_control` (or infeasibility escalation), and **verifiable `closure_evidence`** so the Project Board knows exactly what proof closes the issue.
